    Monsoon-ready output

    Advanced quantum efficiency captures a broader spectrum of low-light wavelengths, so generation holds up on overcast days.

    Built for the heat

    Cells often pass 60 °C in Indian summers. TOPCon's lower temperature coefficient means less power lost in high heat.

    Sealed against humidity

    Dual-glass modules with POE encapsulation keep moisture out, eliminating potential-induced degradation, with no light-induced degradation.

    Light from both sides

    Bifacial cells harvest reflected light from the roof surface, adding up to 10–15% more energy on reflective white roofs.

    Talk to us
    Do we need to invest anything upfront?

    No capital investment is required. Ultra Violet Energies finances, builds and owns the plant. A security deposit equal to two months' subscription is collected at commercial operation and is adjusted against operations and maintenance over the term.

    How is the monthly fee calculated?

    Purely on installed capacity: installed kW × the agreed rate per kW per month, plus GST. It is not a per-unit tariff, so it does not rise or fall with the weather, the monsoon or your monthly load.

    What if the plant generates less during the monsoon?

    Seasonal variation is expected and built into the design. Clear, dry months generate more and monsoon months less. Your fee stays the same every month, which keeps budgeting simple. We continuously monitor performance and keep the plant clean and well maintained.

    How long is the commitment?

    The subscription term is 25 years from the Commercial Operation Date, with a minimum lock-in of five years.

    What happens at the end of 25 years?

    Ownership of the plant transfers to you automatically, with no purchase price. Before handover, we train your team on operation, routine maintenance, safety, monitoring and basic troubleshooting.

    Who maintains the plant?

    We do, for the full 25-year term. This covers preventive and corrective maintenance, cleaning, monitoring, inverter servicing, safety inspections and planned equipment replacement. You remain responsible for your own facility-side electrical system.

    What happens during a grid outage?

    For safety, the plant is on-grid with anti-islanding protection, so the inverters disconnect when the utility grid fails. Your DG set can continue to power your facility through your existing ATS or changeover.

    Can we export surplus power or use net metering?

    Solar power is consumed on site first. Export of any surplus and net metering depend on your DISCOM's regulations, sanctioned load and approvals. We provide the technical support for the application.

    What if we move premises?

    Tell us early and propose your new site. We'll assess it and, if suitable, manage dismantling, transport, reinstallation and recommissioning. Relocation costs are borne by the customer, and the subscription continues through the move.

    How long does installation take?

    About 24 weeks (six months) from project kick-off to commercial operation, subject to approvals, site readiness and material availability.
